# PHOENIXA Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

PHOENIXA ("the app") is a client for Jellyfin media servers on Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV. It is developed by Hanson as an independent project. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary

**PHOENIXA does not collect, sell or share any personal data with the developer or with any third party.** It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no developer-run servers.

## What the app stores on your device

To keep you signed in and remember your settings, the app saves the following **only on your TV device**:

- The address of the Jellyfin server you enter
- Your Jellyfin username and the sign-in token your server issues (your password is **not** stored)
- Your app settings (for example card size, playback options, and your preferred audio and subtitle languages)
- A cache of posters and artwork, so pages load faster

This data never leaves your device except as described below. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes all of it.

## Who the app talks to

The app connects **only to the Jellyfin server you choose**. It sends your sign-in details to that server, along with requests to browse and play your media, and your watch progress (so you can continue on other devices). That server is run by you or by whoever invited you, and its own privacy practices apply. The developer of PHOENIXA has no access to it.

## Data deletion

All data is held on your device and on the Jellyfin server you use. To delete it, sign out, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Data held on your Jellyfin server can be removed by that server's administrator.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
